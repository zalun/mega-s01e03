  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants whose locale writes the day first get day and month swapped (7 April becomes 4 July). This is the bug #4471 describes.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different code: the form’s hint, not the parser. Fixing the parser does not touch it.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a day/month swap. Tuesday becomes Monday, a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto get different days for the same meeting, an invite arrives before the booking. That points at time zones, not date order. The desk spotted these (desk-notes.json) but #4471 still counts them under parser.

How to tell them apart

  • Read the tenant, not the intake tag. meta.intakeTag comes from the reporter’s browser; the “tagged as” column above shows how often it disagrees with the tenant rule.
  • Swap or shift: if the two dates in a report are each other with day and month swapped, it is parser or hint. If they are one day apart, it is one-day.
  • parser or hint: a report through a booking-v3 form is hint. Everything else from a day-first tenant is parser.
